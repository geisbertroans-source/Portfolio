02 · MY PROCESS

From raw footage
to final cut.

I keep the process simple, organized, and focused on making the final video work.

01

Understand

I first understand the project, its purpose, audience, references, and the kind of feeling the video needs to create.

02

Plan

I go through the footage, find the strongest moments, and figure out the structure, pacing, music, and visual direction.

03

Edit

I build the actual edit using cuts, captions, sound effects, transitions, motion graphics, and visual elements.

04

Refine

I review everything, make revisions, clean up the details, and prepare the final video for delivery.
